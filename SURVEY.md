# UX Squad illustration survey — v3

Twelve steps: required name; six product preferences; Home and Two-Wheeler clarity checks at a 64 px slot; one Offers screen comparison; family consistency; final recommendation and optional reasons. No selections by default. Explicit Next, Back and final Send feedback. Mobile options stack vertically.

Product order and A/B/C style assignments are randomized per participant and retained while navigating. Each product keeps its mapping in its clarity question. Family/context order is randomized separately. Canonical style IDs are stored with the presentation mappings.

Responses are identifiable: the participant’s name is saved in the private Google Sheet along with their answers. Names are self-reported, not authenticated. One row is one submission, not proven one unique designer. UUID deduplication protects submission retries, not identity.

Report counts/denominators and neutral responses separately for each product, the two clarity tasks, screen preference, consistency and recommendation. Preserve contrary comments and “Depends on the product.” Do not treat six answers from one designer as six independent respondents.

Limits: original 2D assets are low resolution; no-plinth variants are AI-generated study candidates and can vary in more than the plinth. This compares complete visual directions, not a controlled plinth-only intervention. Internal designer feedback does not establish customer comprehension, accessibility, conversion or universal style superiority. The Offers context is reconstructed from the supplied screenshot; banner and Insta Flexi-cash are constant across treatments.
